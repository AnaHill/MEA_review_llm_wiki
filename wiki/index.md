## Papers

<!-- One entry per ingested source paper. Link to wiki/papers/<firstauthor-year-keyword>.md -->

- [Altrocchi et al. 2023](papers/altrocchi-2023-chronic.md) — Chronic (up to 96 h) FP + impedance assay; 13 reference compounds incl. hERG trafficking inhibitors (delayed effects), TKi, cytotoxics; tolerance-interval QC. Janssen, *Front Pharmacol*.
- [Blinova et al. 2018](papers/blinova-2018-multisite.md) — Blinded 10-site, 5-platform (4 MEA, 1 optical) CiPA study of 28 drugs in iCell2 and Cor.4U hiPSC-CMs; Fridericia ddFPDc/ddAPD90c ("not thoroughly validated for hiPSC-CMs"); logistic models AUC 0.87/0.83; QC criteria; source dataset of Pramudito 2026. Context (2018). *Cell Rep* 24(13):3582–3592.
- [Botti et al. 2026](papers/botti-2026-insilico.md) — 2D bidomain model of a six-well MEA with ventricular-like and atrial-like hiPSC-CM ionic models (30 % atrial-like standard); simulates activation, repolarization, conduction velocity, two Brugada cases and four blockers, compared qualitatively with published MEA data (no new experiments). *J Physiol* 604(19):8141–8166.
- [Clements et al. 2025](papers/clements-2025-aims-poster.md) — ISSCR 2025 poster of Axion's proposed AIMS \#CM01 acceptance standard for spontaneous-beating hiPSC-ventricular-CM MEA assays (spike ≥ 0.5 mV, synchrony, beat period 750–3000 ms, FPDc 200–700 ms with Fridericia). Grey literature, vendor proposal.
- [Correia et al. 2024](papers/correia-2024-acc2.md) — ACC2-inhibitor maturation medium improves metabolic/electrophysiological/structural maturity (patch-clamp, not MEA); in vitro-to-in vivo target-engagement correlation for 21 BCKDK inhibitors obtained only in matured cells. AstraZeneca, *Cells* 13:1339.
- [Dunham et al. 2022](papers/dunham-2022-cardiopyemea.md) — Cardio PyMEA open-source Python tool (PyQt5 GUI; input from Multichannel Systems exports): beat detection, pacemaker origin, LAT mapping, conduction velocity, FPD with a trapezoid-area T-wave endpoint; power-law analysis of pacemaker translocations. UCLA, *PLOS ONE* 17(5):e0266647.
- [Ernault et al. 2024](papers/ernault-2024-interpretation.md) — FP and LEAP interpretation: in NRVM monolayers the T-wave peak tracks APD90 (and the T-wave upstroke APD50) only for biphasic T waves, a minority morphology; LEAP (hiPSC-CM recordings and simulations) is generated by neighbouring-cell current and matches local APD90 only in its terminal duration. *Am J Physiol Heart Circ Physiol* 326:H800–H811.
- [Fetterman et al. 2024](papers/fetterman-2024-maturation.md) — Functional, metabolic, and transcriptional maturation of hiPSC-CMs are induced independently by different medium components; CardioExcyte96 impedance + patch-clamp AP/Nav + Seahorse OCR + RNA-seq. Northwestern, *Cell Reports* 43:114160.
- [Guerrelli et al. 2024](papers/guerrelli-2024-hipscm.md) — Temporal and spatial confounders in hiPSC-CM MEA; 22.6% beating rate drift in 20 min; outer rows 8.8 bpm faster; FPD increases 257 ms over 14 days; KCNJ2 up 27.5× over 30 days. *Am J Physiol* 327:H12–H27.
- [Hayakawa et al. 2014](papers/hayakawa-2014-motion-fp.md) — Simultaneous video motion analysis and MEA field potentials in iCell monolayers; contraction–relaxation duration and FPD linearly related per drug (E-4031, verapamil, isoproterenol), non-arrhythmic conditions; notes that the two FPD end points then in use appear to match different phases of the relaxation motion. *J Mol Cell Cardiol* 77:178–191. Pre-2022 background.
- [Hwang et al. 2023](papers/hwang-2023-spheroids.md) — In silico 3D models of hiPSC-CM spheroids on an MEA compared with in vitro MEA data: FP shapes vary strongly with stimulation and electrode location, so three-dimensionality contributes to FP variability; Fridericia FPDc; E-4031 and nifedipine IC50 values from matched FPD in line with the literature. *Front Physiol* 14:1123190.
- [Ismaili et al. 2023](papers/ismaili-2023-hamburg.md) — Hamburg perspective review: hiPSC-CM If/ICa,T expression, the mixed-population (ventricular/atrial/nodal) debate, atrial models, automated patch clamp; the paper does not discuss MEA, FPD or rate correction. *Front Physiol* 14:1132165.
- [Kabanov et al. 2026](papers/kabanov-2026-cardioscripts.md) — CardioScripts/Myopyth: open-source Python for combined AFM+MEA electromechanical analysis; electromechanical coupling/decoupling; lidocaine validation. CEITEC MU, *Biomed Signal Process Control* 120:110125.
- [Lee et al. 2024](papers/lee-2024-cardiomea.md) — CardioMEA: open-source Python platform for HD-MEA data (parallel processing, SQL, web dashboard, extracellular and intracellular-like features, AutoML feature importance); FPD = R spike to T-wave peak, no FPDc; FPD tracked APD poorly in iCell recordings. ETH Zürich / UZH, *Front Physiol* 15:1472126.
- [Li et al. 2025](papers/li-2025-maturation.md) — Maturation medium (fatty acids, 1.8 mM Ca²⁺), nanopatterning and 2 Hz electrostimulation in 2D iPSC-CMs; MEA CV, spike amplitude and spike slope rise from B27 to MM groups (CV 12.5 → 27.8 cm/s), in line with patch-clamp RMP/Vmax/INa/IK1; B27 cultures arrest with verapamil and lack E-4031 FPDc prolongation; source of Scheurer et al.'s maturity labels; no reference values. TU Dresden, *Nat Commun* 16:2785.
- [Lin et al. 2023](papers/lin-2023-nanoelectronics.md) — Extracellular FP from stretchable mesh nanoelectronics embedded in 3D hiPSC-CM microtissues (± hiPSC-ECs), days 15–37; unsupervised ML pseudotime (PHATE + Slingshot) on whole spike waveforms orders recordings along an electrical maturation trajectory, cocultures further along; depolarization features only (no FPD), one line, scRNA-seq in separate cultures; not an MEA plate, no reference values. Harvard, *Sci Adv* 9:eade8513.
- [Mäki 2023](papers/maki-2023-opinion.md) — Opinion: first positive FP peak is neighbor-cell artifact; FPD end = baseline return not repolarization peak; morphology-aware handling for pacemaker/inverted signals; DatAnalyzer. Zenodo v3 (published 23 June 2026; first version 2023).
- [Matsuda et al. 2025](papers/matsuda-2025-uhd.md) — Sony UHD-CMOS-MEA (236,880 electrodes over 5.9 × 5.5 mm; 11 µm electrodes): field potential imaging, excitation origins, propagation area, conduction velocity; 11 compounds; doxorubicin effects at 0.03 µM within 24 h; no FPD or FPDc in its own recordings. *Toxicol Sci* 208(2):384–400.
- [Maurissen et al. 2024](papers/maurissen-2024-mutation.md) — Isogenic KCNH2 N588D (LQTS) / N588K (SQTS) compound-heterozygous mutations; 3D CTSs with mesenchymal cells required for TdP-like waveforms; FPDcF 82–323 ms across genotypes. *Sci Rep* 14:2586.
- [Park et al. 2025](papers/park-2025-cardiotoxicity.md) — Healthy vs. LQTS (KCNH2 c.453delC) hiPSC-CMs with 8 arrhythmogenic drugs (concentrations from Millard et al.); FPD20/FPDc20 (lowest tested concentration with >20 % change); E-4031 FPD20 0.003 µM in the LQTS line vs 0.01–0.03 µM in healthy lines (3.3- to 10-fold, no statistics); FPDc from vendor software, formula not named. *Korean J Physiol Pharmacol* 29(2):257–269.
- [Park et al. 2026](papers/park-2026-genotype-ml.md) — ANN/RF/XGBoost on MEA FPD/FPDc/beat period/amplitude from LQTS and BrS patient lines plus normal lines, 28 drugs; AUC 0.94 for LQTS-trained ANN; quiescence coded as 100 % FPDc change; overlaps with Park 2025 (same group, probably same LQT2 line). *J Adv Res* 82:381–396.
- [Pramudito et al. 2026](papers/pramudito-2026-ensemble.md) — Stacking ensemble (RF/XGBoost/ANN → meta-classifier) on two ΔFPDc predictors from the public Blinova 2018 CiPA MEA dataset; reports AUC 1.000 on 16 held-out drugs, but resampling gives pseudo-replicates and meta-feature leakage is not excluded; ML runs on pre-computed ΔFPDc, no signal analysis. *Ann Biomed Eng* 54:1334–1344.
- [Ryynänen et al. 2018](papers/ryynanen-2018-single-cell-mea.md) — Single-cell MEA with 80 µm electrodes (opaque and transparent ITO): field potential, beating rate, FPD (Bazett cFPD) and E-4031 effect from single hPS-CMs, with simultaneous video-based motion analysis (not automatically synchronised). *Jpn J Appl Phys* 57:117001. Pre-2022 background; open access.
- [Scheurer et al. 2025](papers/scheurer-2025-optical-ai.md) — Video-based SVM/SHAP classifier separating immature (day 21) from lipid-medium-matured (day 42) iPSC-CM cultures from beating features (99.5 % test accuracy; binary, with a heterogeneous less-mature B27 group); not MEA; cites Hayakawa et al. for the FPD–contraction link. TU Dresden, *Comput Struct Biotechnol J* 27:3719–3728.
- [Vuorenpää et al. 2023](papers/vuorenpaa-2023-mps-building-blocks.md) — Review of cardiac microphysiological system building blocks (cells, matrix, stimulation, functional measurements, computational modelling); multimodal concurrent measurements give data for parameterising and validating models. *Front Physiol* 14:1213959.
- [Weiser-Bitoun et al. 2026](papers/weiser-bitoun-2026-physiomea.md) — PhysioMEA: open-source MATLAB for cardiac organoid MEA; 1D/2D biomarker heatmaps (AMP, Slope, PPD, FPD); BRV with 45 HRV-adapted measures; Brugada Syndrome (SCN5A) validation. Technion, *J Mol Cell Cardiol* 210:137–149.

## Concepts

<!-- Core theoretical or conceptual ideas that recur across multiple papers. -->
<!-- Create a dedicated page only when a concept appears in 2+ papers or is central to the argument. -->

- [hiPSC-CM maturation status as a confound for MEA-derived metrics](hipsc-cm-maturation.md) — Maturation fractionates into independent functional/metabolic/transcriptional axes (Fetterman et al. 2024); maturation status gates in vitro-to-in vivo translatability (Correia et al. 2024); a validated non-MEA maturity classifier exists (Scheurer et al. 2025, video-based, 99.5% accuracy) but no MEA-native equivalent; culture-age electrophysiological drift already quantified (Guerrelli et al. 2024) but not linked to a maturity metric; MEA CV, spike amplitude and slope differ between the media that define Scheurer's labels (Li et al. 2025), without reference values; unsupervised ML pseudotime on extracellular waveforms from tissue-embedded electronics in 3D (Lin et al. 2023) is the closest FP-based maturity analysis found.

- [Multimodal measurement of hiPSC-CMs](multimodal-measurement.md) — Where MEA is combined with impedance, intracellular-like electroporation signals, AFM, video, Ca²⁺ imaging or traction force (Altrocchi, Lee, Kabanov, Hayakawa, Ryynänen, Vuorenpää); the sensor-fusion hypotheses themselves are only in main.md.
- [CiPA in the reviewed papers](cipa.md) — How Blinova 2018, Park 2025/2026, Pramudito 2026, Clements 2025 and Matsuda 2025 relate to the Comprehensive in vitro Proarrhythmia Assay.
