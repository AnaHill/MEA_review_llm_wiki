# Draft

The rules of AGENTS.md apply throughout. Every factual claim in `main.md` must be traceable to a page in `wiki/papers/`.

## Steps

1. **Read `main.md`** to see what is already written, then the wiki pages for the section and any related entries in `notes.md` (cite a note as the researcher's, never as a source).
2. **Edit only the section asked about**, surgically. If you notice that another section should change, ask before touching it. Do not restructure the file unless asked.
3. **When correcting a claim, re-read the whole sentence** and rewrite it if needed, not only the wrong part (a correction appended to a sentence once left its beginning saying the opposite). Then re-read the title, the Highlights and the Abstract, which restate claims in their own words and do not change with them.
4. **Source every claim.** A claim without a source gets `[CITATION NEEDED]`. A statement that is deliberately not sourced — a generalisation over several studies, a reasoned step, the author's hypothesis — is not a missing citation: register it (see "Claims file" below). Do not mark it in the text.
5. **Write back to the wiki.** When the edit compares sources, gathers a theme or introduces a concept the wiki does not carry yet: add the missing link, with a short reason, to the "See also" of the paper pages involved or to the concept page for the theme; give a recurring theme its own concept page; add a tag to the Tag vocabulary only when it fits at least three pages.
6. **Keep the claims file in step** (below), then run the scripts: `python -I scripts/claim_quotes.py` (read the Notes), `--write`, `python -I scripts/check.py`, `python -I scripts/check_abbreviations.py`.
7. **Append the log entry** `## [YYYY-MM-DD] draft | <section>`, naming the wiki pages changed in step 5.

**Done when:** the scripts report no ERROR, the claims-file Notes show no claim left without sentences, and the log entry is written.

## Citations and the reference list

In-text citations are APA: (Smith, 2023), (Smith & Jones, 2023), (Smith et al., 2023), several separated by semicolons; narrative: Smith et al. (2023) found… When two first authors share a surname but the years differ, leave the initials out (Lee et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026), a deliberate simplification for this preprint.

The `# References` list follows the References rule of AGENTS.md; `check.py` reports differences and cited-but-unlisted entries as errors. A source that is not yet in `wiki/references.md` is ingested first.

## Abbreviations and italics

Spell out every abbreviation at its first use in the running text and list it under `# Abbreviations` (after the Highlights). Names of tools, databases, genes and mutations are not abbreviations (`IGNORE` in `scripts/check_abbreviations.py`). Abbreviations are upright; gene names are italic (*KCNH2*); symbols with subscripts use `<sub>` (I<sub>K1</sub>); Latin terms such as in vitro stay upright.

## Claims file

`docs/claims-by-source.md` lists every claim of `main.md` per source, and its last section registers the author's own inferences. When a claim changes, reword it there, note the change and its date; when a claim is dropped or merged, renumber and update every reference to its number. Every new unsourced statement needs a register item with its wording in `main.md`, the sources it builds on and a **Support** value. Before editing the file, after a structural edit of `main.md` (a new or renamed heading, a new source), or when the Notes report something, read [claims-file.md](claims-file.md).
